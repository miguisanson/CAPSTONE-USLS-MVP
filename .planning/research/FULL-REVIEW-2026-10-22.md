## (a) Verdict in plain words

1. The app is in much better shape than the owner's complaints suggest: the four role homes now share one layout, the six
   process boards share one board component, the LOA workflow runs cleanly from student → staff → Dean, and the API refuses
   every wrong-role action I tried (student, faculty, coordinator). The foundations work.
2. What still looks "messy" is that about a third of the screens never moved onto the new system: the six Lifecycle Workflow
   pages (Handoff, Research Gate, Panel Matching, Defense Scheduling, Practicum, Graduation) keep an older header, older
   board and older wording, so a user walking from "Leave of Absence" to "Graduation" feels like two different products.
3. Three workflow pages (Research Gate, Panel Matching, Defense Scheduling) open empty and make staff type a student's name
   before anything appears; there is no list of who is waiting at that step. That is the single biggest usability gap.
4. The demo data undermines the demo: the Dean has nothing to decide on a fresh seed, one faculty advises 22 students against
   a limit of 5, a student's adviser also sits on his panel (which the app itself forbids), students at "Data Collection"
   have 0 completed units, and every event is dated the seed day. The panel will notice before they notice the UI.
5. Two demo-day risks are real: anyone on the same Wi-Fi can reset a demo student without logging in, and five wrong
   passwords lock the shared `staff@usls.edu.ph` account for everyone for five minutes (this happened to my own probe).
6. Reports are still the 19-tab wall the owner complained about (IM20 is decided but not built); the Dean's analytics show
   zeros while the Dean's home says "2 decided this week".
7. Decisions do not notify the student: after the Dean approved the leave, the student's bell stayed at 0.
8. Admin is called a superuser but is refused by half the workflow steps ("must be completed by Graduate School Staff").
9. Nothing I found is a security hole in the "real data" sense (ownership checks on files are correct, CSRF is covered by
   SameSite=Lax + JSON, uploads are type- and size-checked); the risks are demo-mode conveniences.
10. Recommended order: data/seed first (cheap, highest panel impact), then finish the design-system migration of the six
    workflow pages, then rosters for the three picker pages, then Reports (IM20), then notifications and the small bugs.
