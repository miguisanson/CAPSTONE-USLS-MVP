# How to add your voice to the demo videos

Uses only apps already on Windows 11: **Media Player** (plays the video with your lines on screen),
**Sound Recorder** (records your mic) and **Clipchamp** (puts voice and video together). About 10 minutes per video.

The folder `videos\` has one `.mp4` per part and an `.srt` with the same name. The `.srt` is your teleprompter:
it shows each line exactly when you should say it. The same lines, with times, are in `NARRATION-SCRIPT.pdf`.

## Before you start (once)
1. Plug in the mic. Right-click the speaker icon (bottom right) > **Sound settings** > Input: pick your mic.
   Say something: the input bar should move.
2. Use a quiet room. Phone on silent. Headphones on (so the video's silence and your voice don't echo).

## For each video
**A. Practise once (2 minutes)**
1. Open the `videos` folder, right-click the `.mp4` > **Open with** > **Media Player**.
2. If the lines do not show at the bottom: click the **subtitles (CC)** button in the player > choose the `.srt`
   file with the same name.
3. Watch it once and read the lines out loud. Know where you breathe.

**B. Record (one take)**
1. Open **Sound Recorder** (Start menu, type "Sound Recorder").
2. Put Media Player and Sound Recorder side by side. Rewind the video to 0:00.
3. Click **Record** in Sound Recorder, then immediately **Play** in Media Player.
4. Read each line when it appears. A small mistake: pause, say the sentence again, keep going (you can cut it later).
5. When the video ends, click **Stop**. The recording is saved in `Documents\Sound recordings` (right-click it in
   Sound Recorder > **Show in folder**). Rename it like the video, e.g. `2d-Monitoring-Sheet-voice.m4a`.

**C. Put voice and video together**
1. Open **Clipchamp** (Start menu). **Create a new video**.
2. **Import media** > choose the `.mp4` and your voice file.
3. Drag the video onto the timeline, then drag the voice file onto the track under it. Push both to start at 0:00.
4. Play it. If your voice is a little early or late, drag the voice track left or right until it matches.
   To cut a mistake: click the voice track at the spot, **Split** (scissors), delete the bad piece, close the gap.
5. **Export** > **1080p**. Save it next to the original, e.g. `2d-Monitoring-Sheet-FINAL.mp4`.

Repeat for every video: 2d, 2h-1, 2h-2, 2h-3, 2l, 2m.

## Tips
- Speak a bit slower than feels natural, and smile — it is audible.
- Say the role every time ("the student", "the Dean", "Graduate School staff"), exactly as the lines are written.
- If one line is too long for its moment, shorten it as you speak. The video does not wait.
- Keep the FINAL files in this folder. For the meeting, play them from here; nothing else is needed.
