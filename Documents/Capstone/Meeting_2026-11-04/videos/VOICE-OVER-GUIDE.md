# How to add your voice to the demo video

One video, one take: `Full-Demo.mp4` (about 16 minutes) runs all six parts in order (Monitoring Sheet,
Leave of Absence, Readmission, AWOL, Reports & Analytics, Best Features), with a short fade between parts.
`Full-Demo.srt` is your teleprompter: it shows each line exactly when you should say it. The same lines, with
times, are in `NARRATION-SCRIPT.pdf`.

Uses only apps already on Windows 11: **Media Player** (plays the video with your lines on screen),
**Sound Recorder** (records your mic) and **Clipchamp** (puts voice and video together).

## What is in this folder
- `Full-Demo.mp4` + `Full-Demo.srt` — the video to narrate, and its lines.
- `NARRATION-SCRIPT.pdf` — every line with its start time, to practise from.
- `Parts\` — the six parts on their own (same lines, each with its own `.srt`), if you ever need to redo one part.
- `Archive\` — the old voice-overs from the morning. Outdated screens; do not use.

## Before you start (once)
1. Plug in the mic. Right-click the speaker icon (bottom right) > **Sound settings** > Input: pick your mic.
   Say something: the input bar should move.
2. Use a quiet room. Phone on silent. Headphones on (so the video and your voice don't echo).

## Practise (optional, 5 minutes)
Open `NARRATION-SCRIPT.pdf` and read it out loud once. Each part starts with a linking line ("Next, we have…"),
so the take flows from one part into the next.

## Record (one take)
1. Right-click `Full-Demo.mp4` > **Open with** > **Media Player**. If the lines do not show at the bottom, click the
   **subtitles (CC)** button and choose `Full-Demo.srt`.
2. Open **Sound Recorder** (Start menu, type "Sound Recorder"). Put both windows side by side. Rewind the video to 0:00.
3. Click **Record** in Sound Recorder, then immediately **Play** in Media Player.
4. Read each line when it appears. A small mistake: pause, say the sentence again, keep going (you can cut it later).
   Need a break? Pause both at a fade between parts, then press both again.
5. When the video ends, click **Stop**. Rename the recording `Full-Demo-voice.m4a`
   (right-click it in Sound Recorder > **Show in folder**).

## Put voice and video together
1. Open **Clipchamp** > **Create a new video** > **Import media** > choose `Full-Demo.mp4` and your voice file.
2. Drag the video onto the timeline, the voice onto the track under it. Both start at 0:00.
3. Play it. If your voice is a little early or late, drag the voice track until it matches.
   To cut a mistake: click the voice track at the spot, **Split** (scissors), delete the bad piece, close the gap.
4. **Export** > **1080p**. Save it here as `Full-Demo-FINAL.mp4`.

## Tips
- Speak a bit slower than feels natural, and smile — it is audible.
- Say the role every time ("the student", "the Dean", "Graduate School staff"), exactly as the lines are written.
- If one line is too long for its moment, shorten it as you speak. The video does not wait.
