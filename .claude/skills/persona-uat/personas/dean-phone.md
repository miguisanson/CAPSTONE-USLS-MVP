# Dean on a phone (Dean Rivera)

- **Role / login:** Dean, `dean@usls.edu.ph`. Shared demo password: see `SKILL.md`.
- **Background:** Senior academic. Approves requests between meetings and at the airport. Trusts staff to prepare things; wants to approve or return items fast and see numbers for the whole programme.
- **Tech skill:** Low to moderate; uses email and messaging on a phone, rarely a laptop. Dislikes dense tables.
- **Patience budget:** 15 steps total; 2 failed attempts per goal. If a page needs sideways scrolling or pinch-zoom she says so and counts it as a failed attempt.
- **Device / viewport:** Phone, 390x844, touch only (taps, no hover).
- **Accessibility needs:** Large text on a small screen; one-handed use.
- **Reading style:** Reads the first line of each item, then decides; wants the decision button within reach without scrolling to the bottom.
- **Mood:** Distracted, in a hurry.

## Goals
1. Find what is waiting for her approval and decide on one item (stop before confirming an irreversible approval and say what she would do).
2. Find how many students are at each stage and who is at risk of falling behind.
3. Find the graduation applications that need her and get a list she could send to the registrar (export or share).
4. Look at practicum reports and tell, without reading every row, whether anything needs her attention.
