9 rules from the Graduate School policies

Master's: all requirements within 5 academic years

5 years

A master's student completes all requirements within five academic years from the date of admission. Beyond that the student needs the extension below.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 54 (Maximum Residence - Master's Program)

Enforced by the system

Master's: absolute maximum of 7 academic years, including leave of absence

7 years

A master's program must be finished within seven academic years including leave of absence. A student who cannot finish within seven years re-enrolls all courses taken. The residence clock is not paused during a leave.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 55 (Maximum Residence - Master's Program)

Enforced by the system

Doctorate: all requirements within 7 academic years

7 years

A doctorate student completes all requirements, including the dissertation, within seven academic years from the date of admission.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 55 (Maximum Residence - Doctorate Program)

Enforced by the system

Doctorate: absolute maximum of 9 academic years, including leave of absence

9 years

A doctoral program must be finished within nine academic years including leave of absence. A student who cannot finish within nine years re-enrolls all courses taken. The residence clock is not paused during a leave.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 55 (Maximum Residence - Doctorate Program)

Enforced by the system

Extension beyond the normal limit

2 years

A student who cannot comply with the normal maximum residence is allowed a maximum two-year extension.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, pp. 54-55 (Maximum Residence)

Enforced by the system

Graded refresher course during the extension

6 units

During the two-year extension the student enrolls in a graded 6-unit refresher course related to the area of specialization.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, pp. 54-55 (Maximum Residence)

Enforced by the system

Time on leave of absence counts toward maximum residence

Yes

The maximum-residence limits include leave of absence. The residency clock keeps running while a student is on leave; it is never paused.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 55 (Maximum Residence)

Enforced by the system

Master's: candidacy within 3 academic years

3 years

Candidacy to the degree must be attained within three academic years from the date of admission.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 54 (Maximum Residence - Master's Program)

Documented only

Candidacy status is decided by the Graduate School and is not tracked as a date in the system.

Doctorate: candidacy within 5 academic years

5 years

Candidacy to the degree must be attained within five academic years from the date of admission.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 55 (Maximum Residence - Doctorate Program)

Documented only

Candidacy status is decided by the Graduate School and is not tracked as a date in the system.
