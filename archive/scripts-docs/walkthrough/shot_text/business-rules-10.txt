Adding or changing a subject: rules applied
3 rules from the Graduate School policies

Adding a subject: first week of classes only

7 days

Adding subjects is allowed during the first week of classes (7 calendar days from the start of classes) with the written approval of the Associate Dean. After that, an addition is flagged on the enrollment preview and can only be saved with a documented exception (the Associate Dean's written approval).

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 49 (Registration - Adding of Subject/s)

Enforced by the system

Changing one subject for another: first week of classes only

7 days

Changing one subject for another is allowed during the first week of classes (7 calendar days from the start of classes) only if the original subject was dissolved, the student has a schedule conflict, or the student failed the prerequisite subject.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, pp. 48-49 (Registration - Change of Subject)

Enforced by the system

Conditions for a change of subject

Subject dissolved; schedule conflict; failed prerequisite

The reason for a change must be one of: the original subject is dissolved, a conflict in schedules, or failure in the prerequisite subject. Staff confirm the reason; the system records the exception but cannot see the schedule or the grade.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 49 (Registration - Change of Subject)

Documented only

The reason is confirmed by staff and the Associate Dean; schedules and grades are outside the portal.
