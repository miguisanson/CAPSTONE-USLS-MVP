When you can withdraw a subject

Subject withdrawal is allowed until the end of the second week of classes (14 calendar days from the start of classes), whether or not classes were attended (Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 49). It is not free: 10% of the term's total due is charged in the first week and 20% in the second. After the window a single subject cannot be withdrawn; the student may still withdraw from all subjects (paying the full fees for the semester) or file a Leave of Absence.
