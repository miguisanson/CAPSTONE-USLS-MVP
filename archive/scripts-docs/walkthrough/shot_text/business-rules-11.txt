Enrollment and academic load: rules applied
5 rules from the Graduate School policies

Normal part-time load: lower bound

6 units

The normal load of a part-time graduate student starts at 6 units per semester. The portal shows the load on the enrollment preview; a lighter load is allowed for residency, thesis or final-term students, so it is information rather than a block.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 48 (Registration - Academic Load)

Documented only

The handbook calls this the normal load, not a hard limit; the portal shows it as information only.

Normal part-time load: upper bound

9 units

The normal load of a part-time graduate student is at most 9 units per semester. Above 9 units the student is carrying a full-time load.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 48 (Registration - Academic Load)

Documented only

The handbook calls this the normal load, not a hard limit; the portal shows it as information only.

Full-time load

12 units

The normal load of a full-time graduate student is 12 units per semester. Enrolling more than this in one semester is flagged on the enrollment preview and needs a documented exception before it can be saved.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 48 (Registration - Academic Load)

Enforced by the system

Residency enrollment is only for research, practicum, comprehensive exam or publication

Yes

A student with no subjects to take may enroll for residence only to work on the thesis/dissertation, the practicum/internship, to complete an INC, to take the comprehensive exam or to wait for a publication. A student who simply will not enroll for a semester but still has units to take files a Leave of Absence instead.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 48 (Registration - Residency Enrollment)

Enforced by the system

Removal of an INC grade

1 years

An incomplete grade must be removed within one academic year, otherwise it becomes 3.0 (master's) or 2.0 (doctorate) and the subject must be taken again. A student with an INC in every subject is dropped from the rolls.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 54 (Academic Performance - Incomplete Grades)

Documented only

Grades are outside the system's scope (stakeholder decision).
