Written request with reason and period

Yes

A request for a leave of absence is made in writing to the Dean. It states the reason for the leave and specifies the period.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 52 (Attendance - Leave of Absence)

Enforced by the system

A leave is approved for up to one year

2 semesters

A leave of absence may be approved for a period of one year (two semesters).

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 52 (Attendance - Leave of Absence)

Enforced by the system

A leave may be renewed for at most another year

4 semesters

The leave may be renewed for at most another year, so all approved leave together cannot pass two years (four semesters).

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 52 (Attendance - Leave of Absence)

Enforced by the system

No leave within two weeks before the last day of classes

14 days

No leave of absence is granted during a semester within two weeks (14 days) before the last day of classes.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 53 (Attendance - Leave of Absence, guideline 3)

Enforced by the system

A leave filed in the second half of a semester marks the courses W

Yes

For a leave filed by the student himself or herself during the second half of the semester, the class standing of the enrolled courses becomes W (withdrawn) and no refund of tuition and fees is given. The portal shows this on the request.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 53 (Attendance - Leave of Absence, guideline 2)

Enforced by the system

Minimum semesters completed before a leave

1 semesters

The student must have completed at least one semester before filing a leave.

Source: Prototype rule — pending Graduate School validation

Days after a leave ends before AWOL is proposed

14 days

Leaving without a formal leave is AWOL

Yes

A student who withdraws from the college without a formal leave of absence is considered absent without leave (AWOL) and has registration privileges curtailed or withdrawn.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 53 (Attendance - Leave of Absence)
