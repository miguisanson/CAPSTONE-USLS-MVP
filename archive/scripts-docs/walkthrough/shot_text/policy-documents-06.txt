ANSWER PREVIEW (EXTRACTED FROM THE PASSAGES, NO AI)

Panel question: can a student drop a subject at any time, for example on Day 1? Day 1 is inside the first week, so a student may withdraw a subject on Day 1 with the Dean's approval, but it is not free: 10% of the total amount due for the term is charged in the first week and 20% in the second week. A student may also change a subject or add a subject only in the first week.
Source: USLS Graduate School Operations Manual (draft for validation) (4.4 Withdrawal of subject)
