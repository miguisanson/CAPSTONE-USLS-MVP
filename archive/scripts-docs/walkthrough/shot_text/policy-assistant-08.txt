How many advisees can a faculty member handle in a semester?
The student may express preference for an adviser but the Dean, Associate Dean and Research Coordinator make the final decision based on the degree, expertise, research work, number of advisees (maximum of five (5) per semester), and total workload of the faculty member. In some cases (more advisees but less number of faculty in the program), a faculty member can be assigned more than five (5) advisees.
Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023 (p. 59, 3.10 PROJECT PAPER, THESIS & DISSERTATION - Project Paper/Thesis/Dissertation Adviser)
