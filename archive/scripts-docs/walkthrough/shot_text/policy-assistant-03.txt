Can a student withdraw a subject on Day 1, and how much is the fee?
Day 1 is inside the first week, so a student may withdraw a subject on Day 1 with the Dean's approval, but it is not free: 10% of the total amount due for the term is charged in the first week and 20% in the second week. A student may also change a subject or add a subject only in the first week. After the second week the student may still withdraw but pays the full fees (or fees up to the last month of attendance if the reason is justifiable).
Source: USLS Graduate School Operations Manual (draft for validation) (4.4 Withdrawal of subject)

Is time on leave of absence included in the maximum years to finish a master's degree?
A master's program must be finished within a maximum of seven (7) academic years including Leave of Absence (LOA). A student who cannot finish within seven (7) years is required to re-enroll all the courses taken and comply with requirements to earn new credits.
Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023 (p. 54, 3.6 MAXIMUM RESIDENCE - Master's Program)
