Rules applied on this screen
8 rules from the Graduate School policies · 1 needs review

Comprehensive exam must be passed before the title defense

Yes

A student who passes the comprehensive examination confers with the Dean for an adviser and is advanced to candidacy. Research stages (Form 1 title defense onward) stay locked until the comprehensive exam is marked Passed.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 57 (Comprehensive Examinations - Advancement to Candidacy)

Enforced by the system

Thesis / project paper writing period

2 years

Thesis or project paper writing must be finished within two years from the title defense (or proposal defense). The proposal must be submitted and defended within one year of the title defense.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 58 (Project Paper, Thesis & Dissertation - Standards)

Documented only

The deadline is tracked by staff; the system shows time in stage but does not block on it.

Dissertation writing period

3 years

Dissertation writing must be finished within three years from the title defense. The proposal must be submitted and defended within one year of the title defense.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 58 (Project Paper, Thesis & Dissertation - Standards)

Documented only

The deadline is tracked by staff; the system shows time in stage but does not block on it.

Similarity index of at most 15%

15 percent

The final draft undergoes Turnitin scanning and a similarity index rating of not more than 15% is required before the paper goes to the editor.

Source: Graduate School Research Protocol AY 2024-2025 (Closed-door Final Defense - After the defense (Turnitin scanning))

Documented only

Staff check the uploaded Turnitin certificate; the percentage is not read from the file automatically.

One change of title only

1 requests

A student may request only one change of the title of the study, before applying for the proposal defense.

Source: Graduate School Research Protocol AY 2024-2025 (Change of Title of the Study)

Documented only

Not tracked by the system yet; the panel and the Research Coordinator enforce it.

One change of research adviser only

1 requests

A student may request only one change of research adviser, before the proposal defense.

Source: Graduate School Research Protocol AY 2024-2025 (Request for Change of Research Adviser)

Enforced by the system

Advising contract forms within 5 days of Form 3.1

5 days

Once the research adviser is appointed, the student emails Form 3.2 (Research Timetable) and Form 3.3 (Thesis/Dissertation Advising Contract) to the GS Research Coordinator within 5 days of receiving Form 3.1. The adviser tracker shows the due date and flags it when it is overdue.

Source: Graduate School Research Protocol AY 2024-2025 (Designation of the Research Adviser - II. Advising Contract)

Enforced by the system

Adviser signature waiting: remind after 3 days

3 days

An adviser is reminded when an advisee's manuscript or endorsement has waited for their signature this many days.

Source: Prototype rule — pending Graduate School validation

Enforced by the system
