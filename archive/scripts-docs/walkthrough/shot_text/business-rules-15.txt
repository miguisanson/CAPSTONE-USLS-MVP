5 rules from the Graduate School policies

Subject withdrawal window

14 days

A student may withdraw subjects until the end of the second week from the start of classes (14 calendar days), whether or not classes were attended. The request needs the Dean's approval. It is not free: the fee consequence below applies. After the window a single subject can no longer be withdrawn; the student may still withdraw from all subjects (paying the full fees for the semester) or file a Leave of Absence.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 49 (Registration - Withdrawal of Subject)

Enforced by the system

First week of classes (fee tier boundary)

7 days

Withdrawals filed within the first 7 calendar days of classes fall in the first-week fee tier; days 8 to 14 fall in the second-week tier.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, pp. 50-51 (Fees and Expenses - Refund of Fees)

Enforced by the system

Fee charged for a first-week withdrawal

10 percent

10% of the total amount due for the term is charged when the subject is withdrawn within the first week of classes, regardless of whether classes were attended.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, pp. 49, 51 (Registration - Withdrawal of Subject; Fees - Refund of Fees)

Documented only

The portal shows the fee consequence as information; it does not compute or collect payments (Business Office).

Fee charged for a second-week withdrawal

20 percent

20% of the total amount due for the term is charged when the subject is withdrawn within the second week of classes.

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, pp. 49, 51 (Registration - Withdrawal of Subject; Fees - Refund of Fees)

Documented only

The portal shows the fee consequence as information; it does not compute or collect payments (Business Office).

Fee charged after the second week

100 percent

After the second week the student may still withdraw all subjects at any time of the semester but pays the full enrollment fees for the semester (a justifiable reason limits the charge to the last month of attendance).

Source: Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 51 (Fees and Expenses - Refund of Fees)

Documented only

The portal shows the fee consequence as information; it does not compute or collect payments (Business Office).

When you can withdraw a subject

Subject withdrawal is allowed until the end of the second week of classes (14 calendar days from the start of classes), whether or not classes were attended (Graduate School Handbook 2022-2023, p. 49). It is not free: 10% of the term's total due is charged in the first week and 20% in the second. After the window a single subject cannot be withdrawn; the student may still withdraw from all subjects (paying the full fees for the semester) or file a Leave of Absence.
