How this works
Policy questions are answered only from the retrieved passages of the policy documents.
Every answer lists its sources. Open one to see the document, page, section and exact passage.
If the documents do not cover a question, it says so instead of guessing.
Questions like “what is my next step?” use only your own record or queue.
An “Offline answer” label means Gemini was not used and the answer is a keyword match.
